## Screenshots for the Windows 11 Taskbar Centering mod for Windhawk

`rightdefault.png` shows the crosshair image Paint window to the right of the start button and the app icon on the taskbar is right of the start button.

`leftside.png` shows that movement of the window to the left of the center of the screen also moves the app icon for that window to the left of the start button.

Taskbar icons are oriented by the center of their respective app window to the center of the screen as the origin.
